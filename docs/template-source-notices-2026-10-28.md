## Images and video

Basel and Lumen contain no embedded images or videos. Form & function contains one Unsplash architecture photograph, identified by source asset `photo-1487958449943-2429e8be8625`. Wonderland contains 37 independent images. MetaLab contains 29 image instances and three original video files; its source resource page retains Unsplash, Pexels, and All Hands/Figma credits.

[Unsplash](https://unsplash.com/license), [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/license/), and [Nappy](https://nappy.co/license) publish their own asset licenses. Those licenses apply to assets actually attributable to the respective source. This review did not independently map every Wonderland image or every MetaLab image, composite mockup, and video to an individual rights record. A public Pitch or media URL alone does not establish that mapping.

Source credits remain in the templates. When preparing a deck for distribution, use imagery you have the required rights to and preserve any applicable notices.
