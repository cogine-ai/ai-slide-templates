## Font-distribution fixes

**Cabinet Grotesk is not an OFL font.** [Fontshare's ITF Free Font License](https://www.fontshare.com/licenses/itf-ffl) restricts repository and third-party template distribution, and imposes conditions on extractable document embedding. The submitted Press Kit uses [Manrope under SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/manrope/OFL.txt), rather than distributing the source Cabinet font files. The source frames, palette and grid remain; metrics calibration and the resulting glyph differences are documented. Font substitution does not transfer rights in the source photography, wordmark or slide design.

**Hanson:** the exact source font contained “Creative Commons / Please give credits to author,” with no version or license URL. The [author's Hanson page](https://www.behance.net/gallery/78164191/HANSON-BOLD-%28FREE-FONT%29) identifies it, but a complete redistribution grant was not established. This version embeds [Unbounded under SIL OFL 1.1](https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/unbounded/OFL.txt) instead. It does not distribute the Hanson font payload.

The unchanged exact-font study folders are preserved only in the original workspace's ignored `outputs/restricted-template-studies/exact-font-2026-11-01/`. They are excluded from Git and are not dependencies of the submitted templates or available in a new clone.

**Savate:** this build uses the original static design. Its outlines and metrics were matched against the original [Collectif We.ch source](https://github.com/CollectifWech/Savate), which carries the 2015 MIT license. It does not use the later variable redraw or claim that version's license for the earlier file.

Other included notices apply to the actual files used, rather than being assumed from a family name. The Figma reconstruction fonts carry their recorded official licenses.

## Source design and media risks

Pitch public viewing and public asset URLs do not establish a separate permission to redistribute a presentation, photography collection, or illustration set as a standalone template library. The four Pitch adaptations retain source attribution; standalone source-design/media clearance remains unconfirmed. The [Pitch terms](https://pitch.com/terms-of-use), including the permitted scope of included content and separately licensed media, continue to apply. The code review records this as an unresolved rights risk, not completed clearance.

Figma describes Community slide-deck templates as [CC BY 4.0](https://help.figma.com/hc/en-us/articles/360042296374-Figma-Community-copyright-and-licensing). The two adaptations include attribution and modification notices. This does not establish licenses for every external photograph, brand asset or font shown in the files. Only public single-slide previews were available without account access; no editable Figma document was retrieved. Colors and typography were matched from those previews and are not asserted to be original Figma paint/font definitions.

Independent source photos and illustrations are separate replaceable elements, not whole-slide screenshot backgrounds. The Figma device examples use locally authored fictional Fieldwork screens. Four isolated inspiration photographs in Design Review still lack individual-photographer provenance. The Pitch adaptations retain independent source images and SVG ornaments whose individual redistribution rights have not all been mapped. Long demo prose was rewritten within the original text-frame capacity.

Personal Branding's asset with a GIF-like identifier was delivered to the browser as a single-frame AVIF. The HTML preserves the observed frame; unseen animation was not reconstructed or verified.

Preserve applicable attribution, obtain any necessary permissions and use appropriately licensed media when distributing a derived deck. A review branch or draft PR is still a distribution of its files; review status is not a substitute for asset permission.
