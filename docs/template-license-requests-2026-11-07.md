Could you specify whether such permission is available, its cost if any, exact font files and formats covered, downstream rights, attribution and license retention requirements, and whether embedding, format conversion or subsetting is allowed?

No font substitution is planned. No custom license has yet been purchased or accepted.

Thank you.
