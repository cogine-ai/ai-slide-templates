Why we switch the harbour off

For ten nights the street lamps along the waterfront are dimmed from 18:00, so every installation reads clearly from across the water and the old quay.

Cafés on the quay keep a warm, low glow, and stewards with blue lanterns walk the trail until midnight.

The lamps come back on when the last tram leaves.

Before you go

Getting here

Tram 4 or 9 to Harbour Square, then a five-minute walk. Bike parking at Lantern Gate.

Access

The whole trail is step-free. Quiet hours with dimmed sound run 18:00–19:00 on weekdays.

Entry

Free, no ticket needed. Groups of 15 or more can book a guided slot online.
