Clarity

Say one thing per slide. If the audience has to search for the point, the design has failed.

Structure

Align everything to a shared grid. Order creates calm, and calm creates attention.

Economy

Remove until it breaks, then add back one thing. Restraint is a feature, not a limitation.

Observe

Watch how people actually use the thing. Collect evidence, not opinions.

Reduce

Strip the problem to its essentials. Name the one job it must do.

Arrange the essentials on the grid. Let proportion carry the message.
